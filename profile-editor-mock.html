Writing a role description the AI can use

Free-form prose is fine — aim to touch all eight themes. The coverage chips light up as each is detected.
  1. MissionWhy your role exists, in one or two sentences.
  2. What success looks likeConcrete and time-boxed: "Success this quarter = Duc signs off on the PoC and two more WPs onboard."
  3. What failure looks likeThe inverse — what must not happen. Risks threatening this get elevated automatically.
  4. Relationships & dependenciesWho you answer to, who depends on you, who you depend on. Dependency direction drives blocker routing.
  5. Resources & authorityTeam, budget, partnerships — and what you can approve alone vs. what escalates.
  6. Boundaries — not your jobWhat you explicitly don't own, so the AI routes it away instead of guessing.
  7. Constraints & pressuresBudget ceilings, deadlines, team size, regulatory context — calibrates what a "reasonable action" is.
  8. Time horizonsWhat's a this-week concern vs. a this-year ambition, so urgency inference has a baseline.
